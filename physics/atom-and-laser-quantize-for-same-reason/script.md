# Why an Atom and a Laser Cavity Quantize for the Same Reason — Narration Script (Gate 1)

*Source: Candidate 11, `quantum-mechanics-vol1/chapters/04-the-schrodinger-equation.md`. Arc: Hook → Accumulation → Reveal → Implication. One sentence = one beat. ~12 beats ≈ 64s.*

**Production: mostly Manim, hook + bookends as doodles.** A wave between two walls. A mismatched wave (non-zero at the ends) cancels itself to nothing on its round trip; a fitted wave (zero at both walls) reinforces into a standing wave (move: *transform*). Then only a discrete ladder of modes fits — and that ladder *is* the atom's allowed energies and the laser cavity's allowed colors. Stroke-on transforms, so it renders fast. The hooks are a **person** holding a laser pointer beside a glowing atom, then drawing an equals sign between them (personality → doodle). Bear only in intro/outro. Fitted/surviving waves in accent Warm Slate `#5A5653`; mismatched ends + "cancels" in red `#C0392B`.

| H01 | **Doodle** | A laser emits only certain exact colors, and an atom holds only certain exact energies. | a person with a laser pointer beside a glowing atom |

# Who Observes Schrödinger's Cat

INTRO. Bear's Doodles

Who Observes Schrödinger's Cat

A00. Schrödinger's cat is not really a story about a cat.
A01. It is a story about where observation begins.
A02. A sealed box hides one random quantum event.
A03. The event branches into two possible records.
A04. One branch says the trigger fired.
A05. The other branch says the trigger stayed quiet.
A06. The cat is not separate from those branches.
A07. Inside the box, the cat's experience follows the event.
A08. Fired trigger pairs with a cat seeing the result.
A09. Quiet trigger pairs with a cat seeing nothing.
A10. Mixed records like fired-but-unseen do not belong.
A11. That linking is entanglement, not ordinary ignorance.
A12. Opening the box links the observer to the same record.
A13. Now the observer also belongs to a branch.
A14. Collapse seems to select one shared history.
A15. But quantum theory does not name the final observer.
A16. Maybe one outcome becomes real for reasons we still seek.
A17. Maybe every branch continues inside a larger quantum story.
A18. Schrödinger's cat points at the measurement problem.

# Why One Electron at a Time Still Builds Stripes — Narration Script (Gate 1)

*Source: Candidate 01, `quantum-mechanics-vol1/chapters/02-matter-waves.md`. Arc: Hook → Accumulation → Reveal → Implication. One sentence = one beat. ~13 beats ≈ 2 min.*

**Production: mostly Manim, hook + bookends as doodles.** The experiment — slits, screen, dots piling into fringes — must be exact, so it's Manim (move: *accumulate*). The hook is a person at the apparatus (personality → doodle). The Bear appears only in intro/outro.

| Beat | Register | Narration | Drawn element |
|---|---|---|---|
| **INTRO** | Doodle | "Bear's Doodles" [pause] "Why One Electron at a Time Still Builds Stripes" | title card (Manim text) + bear marker |
| H01 | **Doodle** | Fire electrons at a screen one at a time, so alone that only one is ever in flight. | a person at an electron gun, firing one |
| H02 | **Doodle** | Each should land as a single dot — a particle, with nothing to interfere with. | the person watching one dot appear |
| A01 | Manim | [CUT] Set up two narrow slits with a detector screen waiting behind them. | two slits + blank screen |
| A02 | Manim | The first electrons strike the screen as scattered, random dots. | a few random dots |
| A03 | Manim | After a hundred, the dots still look like meaningless speckle. | ~100 random dots |
| A04 | Manim | But after seventy thousand, the dots stack into sharp bright and dark stripes. | dots accumulate into interference fringes |
| A05 | Manim | Those stripes are an interference pattern — the signature of overlapping waves. | fringe envelope highlighted |
| A06 | Manim | [CUT] Each electron's wave passed through both slits at once and interfered with itself. | one wave splitting through both slits, overlapping |
| A07 | Manim | That self-interference sets the odds of where its single dot can land. | probability envelope over the screen |
| A08 | Manim | Any one dot is random; the crowd of them traces those odds exactly. | one dot (random) → the full pattern (exact) |
| A09 | Manim | A particle too small to split still travels as a wave of possibility. | the wave + a single landing dot, side by side |
| **OUTRO** | — | (spoken) Thanks for watching… | title top margin · channel bottom margin, plot kept |

**CUTs:** H01, H02 (doodle scenes), A01 (experiment), A06 (the explanation). Two Manim scenes keep element counts in check: the screen/accumulation (A01–A05) and the why (A06–A09).

**Doodle objects** (INTRO, H01, H02) use the Bear's Doodles Ale-Giorgini prompt; H01/H02 are a *person* (the experimenter), not the bear.

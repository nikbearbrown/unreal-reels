## Description
Finding every energy of a quantum oscillator looks like a brutal differential equation. But two simple moves — step up, step down — hand you the whole answer.

Picture the oscillator's energy on a vertical axis, with a state sitting at some level. One operator bumps any state up exactly one rung of energy. Another operator bumps it back down, exactly one rung at a time. Keep lowering and the dot marches down toward the bottom. But energy can't be negative — so the bottom rung is a floor the lowering operator kills. From that floor, the raising operator generates every level above it. And every rung is the same step apart — a perfectly even ladder. The whole infinite spectrum, found by stepping a ladder — no equation ever solved.

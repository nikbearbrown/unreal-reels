# Why You Can Solve a Quantum Oscillator Without Ever Solving It — Narration Script (Gate 1)

*Source: Candidate 15, `quantum-mechanics-vol1/chapters/07-the-harmonic-oscillator.md`. Arc: Hook → Accumulation → Reveal → Implication. One sentence = one beat. ~12 beats ≈ 62s.*

**Production: mostly Manim, hook + bookends as doodles.** An evenly spaced energy ladder. A raise operator steps a dot up one rung, a lower operator steps it down; since energy can't be negative there's a floor the lowering kills, and from that floor raising builds the whole spectrum (move: *accumulate*). Deliberate visual contrast to Candidate 12 (whose ladder fans apart as n²) — this oscillator ladder is perfectly *even*. Stroke-on, fast render. The hooks are a **person** daunted by scary math, then relieved holding an up arrow and a down arrow (personality → doodle). Bear only in intro/outro. Dot + raise + ladder in accent Warm Slate `#5A5653`; the floor it can't pass + closing line in red `#C0392B`.

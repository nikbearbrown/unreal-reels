What Wave-Particle Duality Means

A00. Big objects usually give us one clean story.
A01. A marble rolls along one track to one stop.
A02. A ripple gives us the opposite kind of story.
A03. It spreads outward, touching many places together.
A04. Quantum objects mix those two stories in one event.
A05. Before detection, an electron is described by a spreading wave.
A06. That wave is not smeared electron-stuff across the room.
A07. It is a map of where one arrival may happen.
A08. When the electron is detected, the map gives one dot.
A09. Repeat the experiment, and the dots make the map visible.
A10. The wave predicts the pattern, not a tiny hidden route.
A11. The particle is the single click that finally appears.
A12. So duality means wave-shaped possibilities and particle-shaped results.
A13. Imagine a whisper spreading everywhere, then landing in one ear.
A14. Imagine a pond ripple choosing one leaf and missing every other leaf.
A15. Quantum mechanics says electrons really behave that strangely.

## Title
Why Measuring Spin Sideways Erases What You Just Learned About Spin Up — Bear's Doodles

## Description
You sort a pile of atoms until every single one is certainly spin-up. Check them sideways once, look again, and up-versus-down is back to a coin flip.

Send a beam of atoms into a box that measures spin up or down. It splits in two; block the down beam and keep only the spin-up atoms. Send those pure spin-up atoms into a second box, turned to measure sideways: left or right. A spin-up atom is an equal blend of left and right, so this beam splits fifty-fifty. Keep only the right beam: now every atom is certainly spin-right. Send those spin-right atoms into a third box, measuring up or down again. A spin-right atom is an equal blend of up and down, so it splits fifty-fifty all over again. The sideways check overwrote the up-down answer — each measurement resets what the last one knew.

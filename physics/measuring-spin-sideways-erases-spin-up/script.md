# Why Measuring Spin Sideways Erases What You Just Learned About Spin Up — Narration Script (Gate 1)

*Source: Candidate 08, `quantum-mechanics-vol1/chapters/10-measurement-and-the-qubit.md`. Arc: Hook → Accumulation → Reveal → Implication. One sentence = one beat. ~12 beats ≈ 62s.*

**Production: mostly Manim, hook + bookends as doodles.** Three Stern-Gerlach boxes in sequence — **Z → X → Z** — each one *splitting* a beam (move: *split*). One branch gets blocked with a red X, the survivor is relabelled, and the third box reveals up/down is a coin flip again: the middle (sideways) measurement overwrote the first. Drawn stage-by-stage, not updater-driven. The hooks are a **person** who sorted a pure spin-up pile and is baffled when it scrambles (personality → doodle). Bear only in intro/outro. Surviving beams in accent Warm Slate `#5A5653`; blocked beam + the closing "overwrote it" in red `#C0392B`.

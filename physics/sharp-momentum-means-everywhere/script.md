The sum is normalized by the term count so the centre height holds while the edges collapse (verified: edge level falls from 0.65 at one wave to 0.05 at eleven). Companion to the wave-packet velocity video (06) — that one moves a packet, this one *builds* one. The hooks are a **person** proud of one clean momentum arrow, then baffled when the particle smears everywhere (personality → doodle). Bear only in intro/outro.

Why the Sun Needs Quantum Tunneling

A00. The Sun cannot be running on ordinary fire.
A01. A giant bonfire would spend its fuel absurdly fast.
A02. Yet the Sun has shined for billions of years.
A03. Its real engine lives deep in the core.
A04. Hydrogen nuclei squeeze together there under enormous pressure.
A05. If they touch closely enough, they can fuse into helium.
A06. Fusion releases energy because a little mass becomes light.
A07. But two hydrogen nuclei both carry positive charge.
A08. Their electric repulsion builds a steep barrier between them.
A09. Classical physics says most nuclei should bounce away.
A10. Quantum mechanics adds a strange escape hatch.
A11. A nucleus can sometimes tunnel through the barrier.
A12. Each tunneling chance is tiny for one pair.
A13. The Sun contains an enormous number of pairs.
A14. Tiny chances multiplied by the Sun become steady fusion.
A15. So tomorrow's sunlight is partly a quantum leak.

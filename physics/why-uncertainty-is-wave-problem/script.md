Why Uncertainty Is a Wave Problem

A00. Uncertainty sounds like quantum mechanics being mysterious on purpose.
A01. But waves already know this trick.
A02. A long smooth wave has a very clear spacing.
A03. That spacing gives the wave a clear frequency.
A04. But the long wave is spread across the whole screen.
A05. A short pulse has a much clearer location.
A06. Its crests do not repeat long enough to pick one spacing.
A07. So a sharp position smears out the frequency.
A08. A sharp frequency smears out the position.
A09. Quantum particles use waves to describe their motion.
A10. Position comes from where the wave packet sits.
A11. Momentum comes from the wave packet's frequency content.
A12. Narrowing the packet adds many frequencies at once.
A13. Many frequencies mean many possible momenta.
A14. The uncertainty principle is wave bookkeeping.
A15. Quantum weirdness did not invent the tradeoff.
A16. It made the tradeoff unavoidable for particles.

# Why Mixing Two Frozen Quantum States Makes Probability Slosh — Narration Script (Gate 1)

*Source: Candidate 10, `quantum-mechanics-vol1/chapters/04-the-schrodinger-equation.md`. Arc: Hook → Accumulation → Reveal → Implication. One sentence = one beat. ~12 beats ≈ 64s.*

**Production: mostly Manim, hook + bookends as doodles.** A probability cloud in a square well. Each eigenstate's cloud is static; the superposition *sloshes* left-right (move: *slosh*), driven by two clock hands in an Argand inset (upper-left) turning at different rates — the growing angle gap is the interference phase (φ = p₂ − p₁, two ValueTrackers). This is the direct sequel to Candidate 09's "stationary state is spinning": now two spinners beat against each other. The hooks are a **person** who sees one still cloud, then is startled when the mix sloshes (personality → doodle). Bear only in intro/outro. Cloud + fast hand in accent Warm Slate `#5A5653`; slow hand in ink; closing line in red `#C0392B`.

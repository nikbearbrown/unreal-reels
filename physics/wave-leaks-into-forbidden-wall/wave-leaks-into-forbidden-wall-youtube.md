## Description
A ball without enough energy to climb a hill just rolls back down the slope. But a quantum particle's wave seeps into the hillside before it turns back.

Here's the setup: open space on the left, a forbidden wall on the right. A classical ball rolls up to the wall and simply turns back. Now the quantum particle: it arrives as an oscillating wave. At the wall it can't cut to zero — the wave has to stay smooth. So inside the wall it switches from waving to a fading exponential decay. The tail reaches only a finite distance in before dying away. The particle is still reflected — but its wave reached where it's classically forbidden to be. Oscillating outside, fading inside — the wave leaks into a wall it can never climb.
